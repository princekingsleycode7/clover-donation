Who we are

We help children breathe easier, and their families too.

Wellspring supports children with serious medical needs by covering treatment, surgeries, transplants, therapy and ongoing care.

When a child is sick, the bill often becomes a second illness for the family. Rooms are given up, jobs are left, and savings quietly disappear into co-pays, travel and time away from work.

We step in so no parent has to choose between hope and what they can afford.

What we do

Fund critical care

Surgeries, transplants, chemotherapy, therapy — the treatments that can't wait. We move quickly so a diagnosis doesn't have to become a debt.

Support daily life

Transport to appointments, medication refills, rent kept current, food on the table. The quiet costs that arrive with an illness and are easy to miss.

Stand with families

A caseworker who answers the phone, a community that shows up. No family should navigate a child's illness alone, so we stay beside them, start to finish.

Featured story

Am I gonna die?

Amira would always ask the doctor that.

Her eyes filled with tears. Her little voice would shake as she waited for an answer no child should ever have to hear.

“Am I gonna die?”

She is only seven years old.

While other children her age are supposed to be worrying about school, cartoons, birthday parties, and what they want to be when they grow up, Amira is spending her days between hospital rooms, doctors, tests, and waiting.

Waiting for a match.

Waiting for the phone to ring.

Waiting for someone to tell her that her life can finally begin again.

Amira needs a bone marrow transplant.

Her doctors know what she needs. Her family knows what she needs. But knowing the treatment is possible doesn't make the waiting any easier.

Every day that passes is another day her family has to watch their little girl fight something she never asked for.

And every night, there is the same terrifying question hanging over them:

Will we find the help she needs in time?

Her family has already sold what they could and done everything within their power to keep her going. They have raised $12,480 toward the $250,000 needed for her treatment and care.

But they are still far from the finish line.

And that's where we come in.

You don't have to know Amira personally to care about what happens to her.

You don't have to be a doctor.

You don't have to have thousands of dollars.

You just have to be someone who believes that a seven-year-old child deserves the chance to grow up.

  • To go to school.
  • To laugh with her friends.
  • To annoy her parents.
  • To have her first crush.
  • To graduate.
  • To become whoever she dreams of becoming.

She deserves to have a future.

Imagine being seven years old and asking whether you're going to die.

Now imagine being her parent and having to hear that question.

What would you do if she were your daughter?

You would probably do anything.

Today, you have the opportunity to do something too.

Your donation could help Amira's family get closer to the treatment she desperately needs. Even if you cannot give much, your contribution can be part of the support that keeps her moving forward.

And if you can't donate, you can still help.

She is not asking for a perfect life.

She is asking for the chance to have one.
